Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Michelle Artistry ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website michelle-artistry.vercel.app. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our site and the choices you have associated with that data.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you visit our site or submit a commission inquiry:

  • Contact information — name and email address when you fill out a commission form.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, browser type, and referring URLs, collected anonymously via analytics.
  • Cookies — small data files stored on your device to improve user experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to commission inquiries and provide custom artwork services.
  • To improve and maintain our website.
  • To send project updates and delivery notifications related to your commission (no spam).

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data independently:

  • Supabase — for storing artwork and testimonial data. See Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • ImageKit — for image delivery and optimization. See ImageKit Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — for hosting. See Vercel Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

We retain commission inquiry data only as long as necessary to complete and support your project. You may request deletion at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at michelleartistry992@gmail.com.

Cookies

Our site may use session cookies to improve navigation. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies, but some features of the site may not function properly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at michelleartistry992@gmail.com.
